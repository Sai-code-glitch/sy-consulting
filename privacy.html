Privacy notice

This notice explains what SY Consulting ("we") collects when you use this website or our services, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you send in the enquiry form: name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, study plans and any notes you add.
  • Documents you share with us later, if you become a client (for example transcripts, passport copies and financial documents).
  • Enquiry forms are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email-forwarding service.
  • Your document-checklist ticks on this site are stored only in your own browser. We never see them.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry and arrange your consultation.
  • To prepare applications, visa files and other services you ask us for.
  • We share your information with universities, lenders, insurers, accommodation providers or visa centres only when you ask us to, and only what that step needs.
  • We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become clients are deleted after 12 months. Client files are kept for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time, and withdraw your consent to be contacted. Write to syalamn7@gmail.com.
